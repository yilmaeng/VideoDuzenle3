Accessible Video Editor (EVD)

EVD: A Journey in Accessibility

Accessible Video Editor is an accessible video production application created for blind, low-vision, and keyboard-first users. With EVD, video editing, transitions, audio, image and text overlays, subtitles, video layers, slideshows, audio synchronization, accessible recording, and live streaming workflows become far more reachable.

About the App

With OBS integration and AI assistance, even a totally blind user can create strong and creative video edits. EVD keeps accessibility at its core while reaching more people through multilingual support and an improving user experience.

What's New in 5.0?

EVD 5.0 combines much faster, source-conscious video production with an accessible workflow.

  • Hybrid Smart Render preserves unchanged source sections and rebuilds only processed areas, delivering much faster exports with stronger visual quality.
  • Continuous audio rendering reduces clicks, repetitions, and dropouts at edit points; automatic validation falls back safely when needed.
  • Transitions, overlays, inserted videos, external audio, and selected-range exports now work with the hybrid architecture.
  • Lyrics-based local karaoke alignment preserves supplied words and provides accessible line and word timing controls.
  • Description, subtitle, final-review, and Broadcast Room tools gained human-voice matching, camera, guest-audio, and personal appearance improvements.
