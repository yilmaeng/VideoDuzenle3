Accessible Video Editor (EVD)

EVD: A Journey in Accessibility

Accessible Video Editor is an accessible video production application created for blind, low-vision, and keyboard-first users. With EVD, video editing, transitions, audio, image and text overlays, subtitles, video layers, slideshows, audio synchronization, accessible recording, and live streaming workflows become far more reachable.

About the App

With OBS integration and AI assistance, even a totally blind user can create strong and creative video edits. EVD keeps accessibility at its core while reaching more people through multilingual support and an improving user experience.

What's New in 4.0?

The betas released between March 24, 2026 (3.97 Beta) and April 14, 2026 (3.9992 Beta) now form the foundation of the 4.0 main release.

  • The startup experience, empty project view, panel hierarchy, and timeline were refreshed to feel clearer, more readable, and more inviting.
  • The subtitle workflow was simplified, styling moved into a separate step, more accessible visual styles were added, and subtitle AI evaluation plus preview resilience were improved.
  • The text-to-speech preview now plays the mixed result together with the video audio. Audio settings gained a stronger volume boost, mono and stereo conversion, and better accessibility for channel layout controls.
  • Export, slideshow, CTA, and overlay workflows were improved with estimated remaining time, photo orientation detection, left-right rotation, photo description, and output quality fixes. A bug where insertion-list transitions and overlays were missing from the result was also fixed.
  • Accessible recording and update flows were refined with fixes for Meet, Zoom, and Teams progress, localized release notes in the update screen, and a better covered startup state at launch.
