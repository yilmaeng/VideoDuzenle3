EVD iOS Privacy Policy

24 November 2026

EVD iOS is a free video and audio editor developed by Engin Yılmaz. Basic editing does not require an account, API key or permission to share content with external AI.

Files and local processing

Selected video, audio, images and projects are opened, edited and exported on your device. Library files, projects and exports are stored locally. If you choose iCloud, Files or a sharing destination, that service’s terms also apply.

Microphone, Photos and speech

The microphone is used for recording and voice commands; Photos access opens media you select. Supported devices use Apple’s on-device speech recognition or an installed Whisper model. Speech analysis takes place on your device. Local Apple AI features depend on compatible hardware, system and language support.

Optional external AI

If you configure Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic and explicitly allow content sharing, relevant video frames, images, text and questions are sent to those providers using your API key. If a service fails, the next enabled provider may be tried. Providers may associate content with your provider account and retain it under their own policies. Provider terms apply and charges may arise. Entering a key alone does not grant content-sharing permission. You can revoke permission in Settings. Connection tests, model lists and usage checks send the key and technical requests needed to contact the service, not your media files.

Keys and model downloads

API keys are stored in iOS Keychain. Whisper models are downloaded from Hugging Face at your request; your audio is not included in the download request. The server may receive standard connection information such as your IP address. Models use device storage.

Retention and control

EVD does not automatically send your media to the developer or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. Manage local files and keys in the app, and microphone and Photos permissions in iOS Settings. Copies you share, send to external services or back up are managed through those services. The App Store build does not create development-only frame or command diagnostic recordings.

Contact

For privacy and support, contact engin_yilmaz@yahoo.com.
